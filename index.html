WildWatch

A radar and LoRa alert system for animal-crossing corridors on forest roads. A radar module picks up an animal near the road edge, LoRa carries that alert to a roadside signal before the animal is in a driver's line of sight, and every event is logged to a cloud dashboard.

Terra Carta Design Lab — 2024 Global Award Grant-funded by the Sustainable Markets Initiative Incubated at NID Bengaluru Field-tested at IISc Bengaluru

Overview

01

Vehicle-animal collisions on highways running through forest and wildlife corridors are, in most cases, a reaction-time problem rather than a detection problem — by the time a driver sees an animal, there often isn't enough road left to stop. WildWatch addresses this directly: a radar and LoRa alert chain that senses movement near the road edge and pushes an early warning to drivers before the animal is in their line of sight.

The project began as a Terra Carta Design Lab entry from the National Institute of Design, Ahmedabad, and has since moved from a validated proof of concept into iterative hardware development, incubated out of NID's Bengaluru campus and built in collaboration with IISc Bengaluru. This page is the single working record of that development: what phase the project is in, what's shipped, the real field numbers, and the specific engineering calls made along the way.

System architecture

02

The system runs as a sender/receiver pair either side of a stretch of road. Everything downstream — mount design, sensor choices, connectivity — is built around keeping this chain fast and reliable in field conditions, not around a lab bench.

Detection triggers a roadside LED alert for approaching drivers and logs the event to a cloud dashboard, giving a record of crossing frequency and location over time — useful both for tuning sensor placement and for eventually making the case for where corridors need permanent signage.

Funding & milestones

05
2024
Terra Carta Design Lab — Global Award

WildWatch was selected as a Global Award winner in the Terra Carta Design Lab, an initiative launched by King Charles III and Sir Jony Ive with the Royal College of Art, recognising design and technology that address the climate and biodiversity crisis. The award is funded by the Sustainable Markets Initiative, released against progress milestones.
